Step 1 — Play the story, protect the one missable (60–80 hours)

Play through the five story chapters with Jason and Lucia. No gold medals are needed in story missions, so enjoy the campaign — but work on exploration as you go: scan vehicles and landmarks with the WAiNK app, and study new animals with binoculars, because the 100% checklist will want 200 vehicles and 100 species later.

Protect the missable: after the mission Down to Earth in Chapter 1, look for Lucia at Jason's house in the morning. When she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept. Meditate there before leaving Chapter 1 — it is the first of the 18 spots for Positive Mental Attitude, and the only one that can be missed. Make a manual save at the start and end of every chapter; there is no mission replay.

Step 2 — Reach 100% completion (around 100 hours)

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist. You only need the numbers below — not everything in the game.

Step 3 — Finish Strangers & Freaks and the leftovers (10–20 hours)

Complete the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, finish eight Strangers & Freaks strands, meditate at the remaining spots, and pick up any outstanding combat, driving, robbery or miscellaneous trophies. When the last one pops, Vice Got a Price! follows.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy in the game can be missed. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story, so there is no need to rush the cleanup.

No mission replay exists, so the safety net is manual saves: save at the start and end of every chapter (15 slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving while active — keep them off for the whole run.
